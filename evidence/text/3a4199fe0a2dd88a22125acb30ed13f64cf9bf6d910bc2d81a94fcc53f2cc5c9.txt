What does curling have to do with your firm's data infrastructure? More than you'd think.

In this episode, Mike Langford sits down with Kristefor Lysne, President of Terrapin Technologies, for a conversation about the messy data problem quietly working against wealth management firms — and why getting your data house in order is the prerequisite for everything coming next with AI.

Kristefor has spent 30+ years helping wealth management firms aggregate, normalize, and automate the data behind advisor compensation, reporting, and compliance. He's also a competitive curler at the St. Paul Curling Club — and he has the perfect analogy for what bad data really looks like inside your firm.

In this episode:

Why AI doesn't fix messy data — it amplifies it

The brittle spreadsheet trap that breaks when key people leave the firm

What "bad rocks" in curling taught us about hidden data problems

Why clean, governed data is the prerequisite for AI adoption

The difference between a SaaS product and a tech-enabled service

AEO (Agent Engine Optimization) — what's replacing traditional SEO

Who the right firm is for a solution like Terrapin
