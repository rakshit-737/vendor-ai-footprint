Median Total Master Trust Return Continues Positive Performance for Second Straight Quarter in Q2 2019 According to BNY Mellon U.S. Master Trust Universe/BNY Mellon Asset Strategy View®

U.S. equity market outperformance persisted with real estate returns continuing to trail other asset classes

NEW YORK, Aug. 7, 2019 /PRNewswire/ -- The BNY Mellon U.S. Master Trust Universe returned a median +3.45% in the second quarter of 2019, continuing the trend of positive performance established in the first quarter. The BNY Mellon U.S. Master Trust Universe offers peer comparisons of performance by plan type and size. It consists of 493 corporate, foundation, endowment, public, Taft-Hartley, and health care plans with a total market value of more than $2.1 trillion and an average plan size of over $7.0 billion. In aggregate, U.S. Master Trust Universe plans reported a one-year return of 7.09%, lagging its 3-year annualized return of +8.77% and outperforming the 5-year annualized return of +5.94%. For the third quarter in a row, corporate plans outperformed, as public market investment allocations continued to outperform allocations to alternatives. For the second consecutive quarter endowments underperformed other plan types; due to having the largest allocation to alternatives. "Corporate pension returns this quarter were aided by their higher allocations to traditional equity and fixed income relative to alternatives, similar to the previous quarter. This time, however, within their fixed income allocation, the long duration bias also helped corporate pensions, because long duration strategies outperformed broad US fixed income," said Frances Barney, CFA, Head of Global Risk Solutions at BNY Mellon. Additional Q4 Highlights

Less than 1% of plans posted negative results during the quarter

Corporate Plans saw the highest median return (+4.23%), followed by Healthcare Plans (+3.79%)

U.S. equities posted a quarterly median return of +3.79%, versus the Russell 3000 Index return of +4.10%. Non-U.S. equities saw a median return of +3.06%, compared to the FTSE Developed x U.S. Net Index result of +3.39%. U.S. fixed income had a median return of +3.04%, versus the Barclays Capital U.S. Aggregate Bond Index return of +3.08%. Non-U.S. fixed income had a median return of +3.49%, versus the FTSE World Government Bond Non-US Index return of +3.93%. Real estate had a median return of +1.68%, versus the NCREIF Property Index result of +1.80%.
