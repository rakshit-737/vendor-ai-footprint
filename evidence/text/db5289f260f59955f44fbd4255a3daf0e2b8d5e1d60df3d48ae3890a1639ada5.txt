Security Officer (Lower Manhattan)

Position Summary:

The Clearing House is seeking a Security Officer to join our New York office (Lower Manhattan). The Security Officer assists with controlling access to the facility, screens visitors and employees in an efficient manner to expedite their admittance. The person in this role will monitor the site for suspicious behavior, unlawful guests, safety hazards, inclement weather. Receives and escalates as required all communication from employees regarding emergency and non-emergency situations. Actively engages during emergency events to provide safety to TCH employees and assets.

Essential Functions and Responsibilities:

• Patrols and monitors premises to prevent threats to company assets, production, life safety, and ensures compliance with all company policies, procedures, and processes.

• Controls access to facilities by validating visitor enrollments, verifying identities of visitors, facilitating visitor enrollment applications, while providing an exceptional level of customer service to all.

• Responds to alarms as directed by the Security Control Room and assists with investigating a wide variety of security related events.

• Effectively perform required tasks during emergency events.

• Accurately complete guard tours and contribute to investigation reports,

• Outstanding attendance and punctuality while maintaining professional personal hygiene, courteous communication, professional appearance, and awareness of surroundings.

• Must be willing to alter work schedule as needed to maintain staffing requirements.

Qualifications Required:

• High school diploma or equivalent.

• Basic TCH security training; may require certifications (e.g., life safety, AED, CPR, Fire Watch). Certifications may be obtained after the hire date.

• Effective communication and customer service skills.

• Maintain physical ability to perform required duties during emergency events. (e.g., evacuations, fire drills, medical events, emergency response)

• Ability to work during inclement weather events.

Qualifications Desired:

• Effective customer service experience and demonstrated skills.

• Experience interacting with security devices (e.g., badge readers, access control devices, Iris readers, CCTV, computers).

• Intermediate computer skills to include Windows, Excel, Word, and internet.

• Meticulous and organized

• Excellent observation skills.

• Ability to work under pressure and remain calm in emergency situations.

• Willingness to learn and develop.

Physical Demands and Work Environment: Work includes a combination of sedentary and active tasks for successful performance. These tasks may include, but not be limited to, monitoring security controls, escorting visitors, climbing ladders, standing for extended periods of time, assisting with emergency events, and performing guard tours to include interior and exterior checkpoints. The employee should maintain the ability to lift/push/pull up to 50lbs and heavier weight in the event of emergency evacuation conditions.

Additional physical demands include retaining the ability to complete fine motor skills, such as searching and handling equipment, while also maintaining physical fitness to remain capable of performing their duties without undue risk to themselves or others. This position requires the employee to take quick and effective action in case of emergency situations. The employee must be able to remain alert and focused for the duration of their shifts. The working environment is generally favorable. Slight exposure to mechanical-related hazards accompanied by noise, dust, etc. are encountered during guard tours or specific assignments.

Employees in this position will work a rotating weekly schedule that consists of set reporting times. Either a 7am-7pm shift or a 7pm-7am shift. Employees assigned to the NC facility will receive alternating weekend days off. Additional schedule information will be provided by Security Management upon interview or annual reviews.

The anticipated hourly rate for this position is up to $23.00 an hour. Rate will be determined by the role, experience, skill set and location. For eligible positions, discretionary incentive compensation may be awarded contingent on personal and company performance. Our benefits program includes medical, dental, vision, life insurance, 401k plan with company contribution and company match, tuition reimbursement and more.

About The Clearing House: The Clearing House (TCH) is a banking association and payments company that is owned by the largest commercial banks in the United States. TCH owns and operates the core payments system infrastructure in the US and is currently working to modernize that infrastructure by launching a new, ubiquitous, real-time payment system. TCH is the only private-sector ACH and wire operator in the US, clearing and settling nearly $2 trillion in US dollar payments each day, representing half of all commercial ACH and wire volume. TCH has provided critical services to the banking industry since 1853, and today, our core products facilitate services including Direct Deposit of payroll, bill payments, and check image exchange.
