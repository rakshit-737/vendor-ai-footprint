2027 BNY Analyst Program - Delivery (Manchester)

2027 BNY Analyst Program – Delivery At BNY, the Delivery Practice is where strategy becomes action. Through rotations and high-impact projects, you will help teams plan, prioritize, and deliver work that matters—while gaining hands-on experience in process and value-stream mapping, PI planning, capacity and dependency management, governance, change execution, and continuous improvement. You will collaborate with Product, Engineering, Operations, and business teams to connect priorities to Objectives and Key Results (OKRs), spot risks early, and keep delivery moving. You will also explore how AI-enabled tools and trusted data can streamline routine analysis, uncover useful insights, and support faster, smarter decisions. With exposure to the Enterprise Transformation Hub, you will see how enterprise priorities and cross-platform initiatives come together—and help shape the standards, skills, tools, and measures that make delivery work at scale.What you will doBring delivery journeys to life by mapping processes and value streams, uncovering handoffs and dependencies, and identifying smarter ways of working.Help Analyst Program Our 24-month rotational Analyst Program is designed to set you up for success from day one. New joiners take part in a structured induction experience to help them assimilate into BNY, learn about the organization, connect with peers and leaders, and build the core skills needed to navigate their early career with confidence. From day one, analysts are immersed in BNY's collaborative and innovative culture through: Enterprise-wide onboarding and development programming, including an in person immersive induction experience, executive speaker series, skill development, and networking opportunities. Meaningful project work aligned to key business practices and platforms, providing hands-on experience, skill development, and real-world business impact. Structured mentorship and career support from managers, senior leaders, peer mentors, business stakeholders, and dedicated program managers. Exposure across practices, platforms, and teams, building a broad understanding of BNY's integrated business model. Professional, technical, and financial services training designed to strengthen business acumen and prepare participants for long-term career success. A premier early-career experience, backed by senior leadership and designed to develop the next generation of talent for BNY's global workforce.
