            There's volume coming your way! Instant payments have been a part of the landscape for 7 years. With nearly 900 participants on the RTP network and hundreds in the queue and a reach of 70% of DDAs across the country, transactions are being redirected until you connect. Join us for this informative virtual event and hear from experts throughout The Clearing House on the current state of the network, the use cases driving record setting growth, positioning adoption within your financial institution, working with your technology partners to make the connection and what implementation looks like. You will also hear from a couple peer organizations about their journey.
