FSSI, a leading document outsourcing specialist, has been recognized as one of Printing Impressions’ 2025 Innovators of the Year. The award honors FSSI’s evolution as a forward-thinking print and mail provider powered by advanced automation, cutting-edge technology, and a values-driven, employee-owned culture.
For more than 45 years, FSSI has delivered high-quality print, mail, and digital document solutions to clients nationwide. In recent years, the company’s proprietary automation systems, built entirely in-house, have fueled double-digit annual growth. These intelligent tools allow FSSI to manage complex, customized client requirements with remarkable speed, accuracy, and efficiency, proving that traditional print can thrive through innovation.
FSSI’s innovative mindset also extends to its business structure and growth strategy. In 2021, the company transitioned to employee ownership (ESOP) and has since expanded its national footprint through the acquisition of Cathedral Corporation’s Business Services Division. FSSI now operates from its 167,000-square-foot headquarters in Santa Ana, California, with additional facilities in Texas, Florida, and Rhode Island, enhancing capacity, redundancy, and client support across the U.S.
Recent honors include Jennifer Dietz receiving the 2024 Vistage Lifetime Achievement Award, FSSI being named “Large Business Difference Maker of the Year” by the Santa Ana Chamber of Commerce and earning a Bronze Award in the 2024 Sterling Manufacturing Business Excellence Awards for its Florida facility.
FSSI continues to show how a company in a traditional industry can redefine itself through innovation, strategic growth, and investment in people, demonstrating that when print evolves, possibilities expand.
