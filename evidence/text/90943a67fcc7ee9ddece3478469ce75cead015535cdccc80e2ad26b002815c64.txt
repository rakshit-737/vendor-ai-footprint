Migration Services  
Depending on the version AppWorx, UC4, or Automic Automation 
software you have in place, you may need to migrate your existing 
job configurations to the Automic Workload Automation platform. 
Our senior consultants have written and successfully deployed the 
migration utilities needed to mitigate business disruption. 
 
AutomWorx is not just focused on Automic to Automic migrations: 
we have also assisted customers that are considering a switch to 
the Automic platform from alternative job scheduling programs.  
 
In addition to Automic software migrations, our consultants are 
also highly proficient at helping you switch to new operating 
systems and/or database platforms; i.e., Windows, Linux and UNIX 
distributions.
