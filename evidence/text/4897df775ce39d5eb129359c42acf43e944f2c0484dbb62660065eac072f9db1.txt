OUR VALUE
BNY delivers more choice, speed and transparency in how to pay and get paid. A single connection to the RTP® network and FedNow® Service allows users to send and receive irrevocable digital payments, exchange richer payment messages and strengthen controls with AI-enabled anomaly detection, validation services and secure operations.
PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS
A Single Connection
The RTP® network and FedNow® Service are accessible through one connection for instant settlement, 24/7/365 availability and irrevocable payments.
Complete Payment Support
Our instant payment solution streamlines collections and payments, reduces manual work, improves accuracy and provides real-time transparency.
Advanced Payment Security
AI-enabled payment and account validation services verify data against compliance requirements before and after payment submission in real-time, reducing risk and delays.
