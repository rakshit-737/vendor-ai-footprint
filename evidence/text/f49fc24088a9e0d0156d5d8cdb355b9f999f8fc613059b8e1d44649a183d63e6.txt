When it comes to expanding products and services, often times the best first step is to offer ACH origination services to your existing corporate account holders.  We’ll provide a high-level overview of the obligations of an Originating Depository Financial Institution (ODFI), the effort necessary to take this role on, and a how-to for identifying use cases among existing clients and prospects.
