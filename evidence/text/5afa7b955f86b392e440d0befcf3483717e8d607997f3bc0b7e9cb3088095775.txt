Accessibility for Applicants with DisabilitiesAt BNY we remain committed to diversity. As an Equal Employment Opportunity employer, we provide reasonable accommodations for qualified individuals with disabilities. All requests can be submitted directly to our internal accessibility accommodation team at applicationaccommodation@bny.com. Please include your name, the position ID number, a description of your requested accessibility accommodation as it relates to your disability and your preferred method of contact.

PLEASE BE ADVISED: BNY has been made aware that there are several scams to take advantage of job seekers in a variety of ways. Depending on the scam, scammers look to collect confidential information to use for identity theft or try to get money from job seekers during the recruitment process. BNY will never ask for money during the application, recruitment, or on-boarding process. To learn more about the most common job scams, tips on how to avoid being scammed and federal reporting procedures, please visit consumer.ftc.gove/articles/0243-job-scams.
