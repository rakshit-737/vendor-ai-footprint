SkyBridge Opportunity Zone Real Estate Investment Trust Selects BNY Mellon to Provide Fund Administration Services

NEW YORK

BNY Mellon chosen based upon its strength in real estate administration

NEW YORK, March 25, 2019 /PRNewswire/ -- BNY Mellon has been chosen by SkyBridge Capital to be the sole provider of global fund administration services for its Opportunity Zone Real Estate Investment Trust ("SOZ REIT"), a private, non-traded real estate investment trust with a mandate to invest in residential, commercial, and industrial real estate in US Treasury-designated Opportunity Zones in the US.

The win marks another important addition to BNY Mellon's real estate platform. The BNY Mellon Real Estate Fund Administration global platform has grown to $130 Billion of assets under administration, representing close to 80 funds across more than 20 clients serviced by 250+ real estate professionals.

SkyBridge selected BNY Mellon given its strong performance as SkyBridge's existing custody provider, as well as its experience in real estate administration.

Daniel Barile, a Partner and Portfolio Manager at SkyBridge Capital, said:

"Having worked with BNY Mellon for years on our flagship fund-of-hedge-funds product and given BNY Mellon's experience across asset-types, and expertise at fund structuring, they were the clear choice from day one for SOZ REIT."

Alan Flanagan, Global Head of Alternatives for Asset Servicing at BNY Mellon, said:

"We are proud to work with SkyBridge across multiple portfolios, and we are excited to expand our collaboration with them into the real estate investment space. This decision recognizes our growing offering in real estate administration, as well as our ongoing commitment to servicing our clients and driving industry innovation."

SkyBridge CapitalSkyBridge is a global alternative investment manager that provides a range of investment solutions to individuals and institutions, including the SkyBridge Opportunity Zone REIT, multi-strategy commingled fund-of-hedge-funds products, customized separate account hedge fund portfolios, hedge fund advisory services and a long-only equity mutual fund. SkyBridge also produces a large annual conference in the US known as the SkyBridge Alternatives Conference ("SALT").

BNY MellonBNY Mellon is a global investments company dedicated to helping its clients manage and service their financial assets throughout the investment lifecycle.
