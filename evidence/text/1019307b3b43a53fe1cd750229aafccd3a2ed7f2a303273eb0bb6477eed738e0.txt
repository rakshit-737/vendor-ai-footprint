Key Responsibilities Serve as a hands-on full stack developer, directly designing, building, testing, and deploying applications aligned with business needs. Write high-quality, maintainable code across the front end and back end, actively contributing to core project deliverables. Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product, operations, and security to deliver practical, scalable solutions. Lead by example through technical execution, driving best practices in coding, testing, debugging, and deployment to ensure high-quality software delivery. Explore and implement opportunities to streamline the software development lifecycle through automation, modern engineering practices, and intelligent tooling. Participate actively in code reviews, troubleshooting, and debugging efforts, helping resolve complex technical issues quickly and effectively. Contribute to architectural decisions and technology strategy with a pragmatic, builder-focused mindset grounded in hands-on development experience. Mentor and guide junior developers through day-to-day technical leadership, pairing, code review, and knowledge sharing. Ensure applications meet security, compliance, performance, and reliability standards. Help manage project timelines and deliverables in partnership with stakeholders, while remaining deeply involved in execution. Balance leadership responsibilities with direct technical contribution, operating as a doer who helps move work forward. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or related field 7+ years of experience in full stack software development required. Finance domain experience is preferred Extensive hands-on experience in full stack development, with strong proficiency in modern front-end frameworks (e.g., Angular, React, Flutter, Dart) and back-end technologies (e.g., Java, Spring Framework, Oracle, MongoDB, SQL). Strong understanding of REST APIs, cloud platforms, DevOps practices, and modern software development lifecycle processes. AI Agentic process engineering & skills Demonstrated ability to contribute directly in code, troubleshoot issues, and deliver production-ready software in a fast-paced environment. Experience with or strong interest in leveraging AI-assisted development tools, automation, and engineering productivity practices to improve SDLC efficiency and delivery outcomes. Excellent problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills. Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, agile environment with a strong sense of ownership and execution.
