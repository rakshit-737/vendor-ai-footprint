            The NACHA Operating Rules require financial institutions to complete an ACH Annual Audit and ACH Risk Assessment. This full day workshop is for those who choose to perform their own audit and risk assessment rather than outsourcing to an external service provider or who want to be better prepared for the audit and risk assessment. We'll discuss best practices, present ACH Rule requirements for completing the audit and risk assessment, and identify common findings and non-compliance issues.
