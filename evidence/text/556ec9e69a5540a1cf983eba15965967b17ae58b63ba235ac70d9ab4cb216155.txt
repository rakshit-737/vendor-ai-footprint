Islamic Finance

Islamic Finance

Agency, trust and administrative services supporting Shariah-compliant financing structures across the Middle East, APAC and other global markets.

OUR VALUE

As a global leader in securities services with regional expertise, BNY supports a wide range of debt capital market transactions. We work with corporates, financial institutions and sovereigns to help administer Islamic finance transactions reliably from issuance through maturity.

PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS

Agency & Trust Services

We serve key agency and trust roles, including delegate trustee, issuing, paying, security, calculation and settlement agent, supporting Shariah-compliant structures with disciplined oversight and flexibility for conventional structures.

Islamic Finance Structure Support

We support a broad range of Islamic financing structures, including Murabaha, Mudharabah, Musharakah, Ijarah, Istisna’a, Salam and Wakalah.

Account & Transaction Services

We provide account bank, custody, settlement agent, escrow agent, loan administration, cash management and restructuring services, helping to centralize responsibilities and support efficient transaction execution and ongoing servicing.

BENEFITS AT A GLANCE

Shariah‑Compliant Finance Expertise

Experience supporting a wide range of Shariah‑compliant financial structures helps enable efficient administration while adhering to Islamic finance principles and market practices.

Lifecycle Reliability

Consistent support from issuance through maturity helps provide stability, operational discipline and dependable servicing across the life of the transaction.

Trusted Intermediary

As an independent provider without an investment banking practice, BNY acts in the best interests of all transaction parties, supporting transparency, trust and long-term market relationships.
