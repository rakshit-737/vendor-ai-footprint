Project Finance Services

Project Finance Services

A trusted third-party partner delivering the expertise, controls and global reach required to execute complex project finance transactions

OUR VALUE

BNY acts as a neutral, experienced third-party partner across the lifecycle of project finance transactions. We help sponsors, developers, financiers and other stakeholders navigate complex transactions with confidence, delivering disciplined administration, rigorous controls and global capabilities.

PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS

Escrow Administration

Independent escrow administration for project transactions, with secure cash management, milestone-based disbursements and tailored documentation support.

Trustee and Agency Services

End-to-end trustee and agency support for project finance deals, helping manage obligations, communications and key transaction events.

Loan Administration Support

Administrative support for intercreditor arrangements and loan facilities, helping coordinate documentation, payments and lender requirements.

BENEFITS AT A GLANCE

Risk-Focused Controls

As a neutral third party, we safeguard stakeholder interests through strong financial controls, compliance monitoring and precise funds administration.

Enhanced Transparency

Clear reporting, defined controls and independent oversight promote transparency across stakeholders, supporting trust, accountability and informed decision‑making.

Scalable Global Reach

Our global presence and resilient infrastructure enable consistent service delivery across markets, helping clients scale project finance strategies with confidence.
