Automic SaaS: Modern Enterprise Automation

Automic SaaS

In today’s fast-paced digital world, efficient and reliable automation is essential for businesses to remain competitive. Automic SaaS offers a powerful solution that streamlines and simplifies automation across your entire enterprise, from mainframe to distributed and hybrid cloud environments.

Key Features and Benefits

Unified Automation: Automic SaaS provides a centralized platform for managing and controlling automation processes across diverse IT landscapes.

SaaS Delivery Model: By leveraging the SaaS model, you can offload the management of the underlying infrastructure, allowing you to focus on core business objectives.

Predictable Costs: Enjoy subscription-based pricing, ensuring predictable costs and eliminating the need for upfront capital investments.

Rapid Innovation: Benefit from faster access to the latest features and updates, enabling you to stay ahead of the curve.

Scalability and Flexibility: Automic SaaS dynamically adapts to your changing workload demands, ensuring optimal performance.

Core Components and Capabilities

Object-Oriented Design: Create and reuse reusable objects for efficient task management and process automation.

Automation Engine Scripting: Leverage the powerful scripting language to customize and automate complex workflows.

Automatic Processing (Executing): Easily trigger and execute automated processes, both manually and automatically.

Monitoring and Auditing: Gain comprehensive visibility into your automation processes with robust reporting and auditing capabilities.

Analytics: Transform raw data into actionable insights using advanced analytics tools.

Service Orchestration: Coordinate complex processes across multiple domains and applications with ease.

Integrations: Seamlessly integrate with various systems and applications to create a unified automation ecosystem.

How Automic SaaS Works

Automic SaaS simplifies automation by providing a single point of control for managing and orchestrating processes across your entire IT infrastructure. Here’s a breakdown of how it works:

Object Creation: Define reusable objects, such as jobs, workflows, schedules, and calendars, to represent specific tasks or processes.

Process Design: Assemble objects into workflows to create automated sequences of tasks.

Scheduling: Set up schedules to trigger automated processes at specific times or intervals.

Execution: Execute processes manually or automatically.

Monitoring and Auditing: Track and analyze process execution data for insights and compliance.

Analytics: Use analytics tools to transform data into actionable insights and make informed decisions.

Automic SaaS is a powerful solution that can help businesses streamline their operations, reduce costs, and improve efficiency. By leveraging its comprehensive features and capabilities, you can automate complex processes, gain valuable insights, and drive innovation.
