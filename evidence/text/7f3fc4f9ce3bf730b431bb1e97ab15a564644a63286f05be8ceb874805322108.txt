Volante Technologies Offers Free Cloud-Based Real-Time Payment Processing
Volante Technologies, a provider of payment processing software and services, has been involved with the growth of the RTP network since the network launched in 2017. Today, Volante is leveraging its expertise and is helping financial institutions of all sizes to connect to the RTP network through its free cloud-based real-time payment processing service.
When the RTP network launched in 2017, Volante worked with BNY Mellon to create and deploy technology to enable real-time payments in the US and internationally using VolPay. VolPay worked behind the scenes when the first ever transaction was made on the RTP network on Nov. 13, 2017. Volante’s cloud service, based on VolPay, enables depository institutions to realize the business opportunities of real-time/instant payments without the high upfront infrastructure and software costs and lengthy complex integration projects, traditionally associated with new payment system implementations.
“We have always been passionate about bringing simplification and automation of payments processing to financial institutions,” said Deepak Gupta, Global Head of Payments as a Service, at Volante Technologies. “Our free processing service, with onboarding in 60 days or less, now allows U.S. banks of all sizes to enjoy the benefits of real-time payments.”
