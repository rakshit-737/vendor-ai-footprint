            Have you been using the “checklist” contained in the Nacha Operating Rules former Appendix Eight as a guide for your ACH audit work papers? Appendix Eight was eliminated, are you asking yourself, “What is required to be in the audit?” Do you need guidance on how to address this audit gap? If you answered “yes” to any of these questions, this webinar will help provide a cure for your Appendix Eight pain points. 
Attend this webinar and learn some highly recommended steps to reworking your audit processes and learn how to transform the ACH audit process into one that reviews all of the Rules and then some!
Gain Insight & Understanding
During this webinar, the speakers will provide an in-depth look at the following:
• The history of Appendix Eight
• What the ACH audit requirements
• What should the ACH audit include that is beyond what is found in the ACH Rules
• The top 10 cures for “appendicitis”
