BSA AML Compliance

Member Fee: $209 per person Non-Member Fee: $309 per person Audience: BSA Investigators_Analysts, BSA Officers, Risk Managers

Eagle Eye Golf Club 15500 Chandler Rd. E. Lansing, MI 48823 This session is for those who have the responsibility of the Bank Secrecy Act/Anti-Money Laundering programs for their financial institution. Our presenter, Nancy Lake from ACBB’s Compliance Anchor will share her expertise in BSA/AML, including in what every financial institution should know and the tools needed to implement and maintain a comprehensive BSA/AML plan. Tired of the same old BSA lecture? This is your chance to discuss ways to enhance your BSA program and collaborate with other financial institutions to see how they are developing Risk Assessments, managing the SAR process, and conducting due diligence reviews.

Topics include:

Account Opening

Account Opening

CDD/EDD

CDD/EDD

SARs

SARs

Risk Assessments

Risk Assessments

Beneficial Ownership

Beneficial Ownership

Human Trafficking

Human Trafficking

Participants are encouraged to bring sample forms to share and/or discuss with the group. Examples include: Account Opening Forms, BSA/AML Risk Assessment.

Discounted rates will appear when signed in as a TCHPA FI Member or Corporate Subscriber.
