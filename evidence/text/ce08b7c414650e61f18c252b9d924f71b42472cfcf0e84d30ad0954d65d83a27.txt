            The Card Payments industry is a vibrant, evolving and sometimes scary one.  Some changes seem to happen almost overnight, while others don’t ever seem to move as predicted.  In this session we'll explore some of the most recent trends occurring in card payments and try to put them into a proper context.  This webinar will address the latest mobile payments developments, innovations in authentication and other risk mitigation, interchange trends, an update on EMV deployment and how the regulatory environment is affecting the industry.
