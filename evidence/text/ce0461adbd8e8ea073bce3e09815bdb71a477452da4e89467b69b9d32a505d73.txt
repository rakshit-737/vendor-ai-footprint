Financial institutions can be exposed to a variety of risks whether originating or receiving ACH transactions or outsourcing these activities to a third party. We’ll outline the key components of an effective ACH risk management program and address the various areas of ACH risk (Credit, Fraud, Operations, Systemic and Compliance) and controls.
