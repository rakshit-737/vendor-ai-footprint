2018 RTP Industry Webinar Series
RTP Compliance Implications
Thursday, April 5, 2018 | 1:00 - 2:00 pm ET
Real-time payments add new opportunities and complexities for financial institutions. Integrating RTP into your institutions current compliance program is a necessity. Join us for this webinar and gain an understanding of the compliance issues that participants will need to take into consideration as they implement RTP. Ultimately, each financial institution will need to develop a plan for ensuring RTP is part of their compliance program. Join us for this informative program and start thinking about RTP and your compliance program now.
