            AlphaBay and Hansa are gone, but many other dark websites still remain. Information and products available on the dark web are continuously growing and with the increased number of data breaches in the past few years, more and more personal information is bound to end up for sale. What can you do about it? 
Industry experts demonstrate the ugly side of electronic commerce on the dark web as they take you on a journey where you can buy debit card data, bank account data, and counterfeit currency. Becoming aware of these sites will help your team educate employees, conduct research into potential illegal activity, and complete better due diligence on customers and Third-Party Payment Processors.
Speakers will deliver:
    - A live demonstration of a dark site and reveal the types of information available. 
- Sound business practices for financial institutions and businesses.
- How to educate your customers to ensure they’re updated on how best to protect their information.
