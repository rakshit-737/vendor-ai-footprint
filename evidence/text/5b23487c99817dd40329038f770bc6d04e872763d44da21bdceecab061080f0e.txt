BNY Mellon Awarded Fund Administrator of the Year by Global Investor Magazine

BNY Mellon, a global leader in investment management and investment services, was honoured last week by being awarded Fund Administrator of the Year at the 2018 Global Investor awards.

BNY Mellon was also ranked first overall in the Americas in Global Investor’s Global Custody Survey, as well as first in the survey in the Americas for mutual funds and for multi-custodian.

Hani Kablawi, CEO of BNY Mellon Asset Servicing, said, “These results are recognition of our ongoing commitment to product innovation and service excellence. This is a client-driven survey outcome and I would like to thank everyone within our Asset Servicing business globally for their dedication and hard work."

Global Investor Group's Tim Willmott (left) with Jeremy Paul Shaw of BNY Mellon Asset Servicing

BNY Mellon Asset Servicing offers clients worldwide a broad spectrum of specialised asset servicing capabilities, including custody and fund services, securities lending, performance and analytics, and execution services. BNY Mellon’s fund administration services – including financial reporting, tax services, compliance and expense budgeting – help clients increase efficiencies and address evolving regulatory issues.
