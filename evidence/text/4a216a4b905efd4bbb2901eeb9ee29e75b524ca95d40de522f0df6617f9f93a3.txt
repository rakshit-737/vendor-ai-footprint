Preparing For Your Interview

Get a head start by becoming familiar with our principles, which guide how we behave, how we act, how we treat each other and how we show up for our clients. This will help you prepare as you consider how you can bring your ideas to #LifeAtBNY.

Be Client-Obsessed.

We are here to enable our clients’ success. We don't wait for them to call - we are commercial, we anticipate needs, we obsess about their experience, and we proactively connect the dots across BNY.

Own It.

We know our stuff, take a hands-on approach and dive into the details where it matters. We bridge silos and do what’s best for the firm, thinking about the whole, not just the task in front of us. We play to win and own the outcome.

Thrive Together.

Our culture is built by all of us, and we lead by example. Doing the right thing matters and trust is earned. Creating an environment where everyone belongs is essential – that’s how we succeed.

Spark Progress.

We run to problems, make quick and thoughtful decisions and deliver high quality work. We relentlessly move forward, step by step, to improve BNY every single day.

Stay Curious.

Innovation and learning are core to who we are. We constantly invent better ways to work and are agile by design. Things happen – and when they do, we explore solutions, embrace feedback and take action.

FAQS

Frequently asked questions from candidates.

The most efficient way to express interest in an open position is to search our openings by keyword, location, experience level and job category here, then apply to the position or upload your resume to get matched with job recommendations based on your experience and skills.

BNY job postings are updated every day.

When you apply to a position, you’ll receive a confirmation email. Within the email is a link to access your candidate profile page where you can view your past and current applications, along with the status of each.

After an assessment of your skills and experience, a team member may reach out to schedule a preliminary interview. Each open position has different timelines, but if your status is active and you have not heard back from us, this means you are still in consideration. If it is determined that you do not meet the minimum qualifications, you will be notified via email by a member of our talent acquisition team.

No password, no problem. To make your application experience as easy as possible, we don’t use passwords which can be easily forgotten. Instead, we send a unique code to the email address you have on file each time you log in.

At BNY we remain committed to diversity. As an Equal Employment Opportunity employer, we provide reasonable accommodations for qualified individuals with disabilities. All requests can be submitted directly to our internal accessibility accommodation team at applicationaccommodation@bnymellon.com. Please include your name, the position ID number, a description of your requested accessibility accommodation as it relates to your disability and your preferred method of contact.
