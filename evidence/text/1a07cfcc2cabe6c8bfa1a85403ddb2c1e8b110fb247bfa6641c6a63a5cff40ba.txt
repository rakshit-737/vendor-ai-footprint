This session is intended for non-financial institution corporate payments professionals.
Attendees will be introduced to the Automated Clearing House (ACH) network, its participants, and the Nacha Operating Rules & Guidelines ("ACH Rules") which govern the ACH network.  Topics discussed include:
    - Navigating the ACH Rules 
- Network Participants and Responsibilities
- Rules Enforcement
- Types of ACH Transactions (Standard Entry Class (SEC) Codes)
- Returns, Notifications of Change and Prenotifications
- ACH Use Cases for Consumers and Corporates
