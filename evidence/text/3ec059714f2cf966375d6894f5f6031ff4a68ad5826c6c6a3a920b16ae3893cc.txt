Specialist, Client Processing

We’re seeking a future team member for the role of Specialist, Client Processing to join our GSS Enablement team. Maintain comprehensive documentation, procedures, and evidence of controls whilst contributing to operational metrics and reporting.Identify process improvement opportunities such as automation and workflow enhancements, and explore AI integration to reduce manual touchpoints.
