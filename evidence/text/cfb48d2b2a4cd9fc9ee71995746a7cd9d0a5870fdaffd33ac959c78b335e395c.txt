BNY Announces 2026 Managing Director Promotions

BNY is proud to announce its newest class of Managing Directors (MDs), who will be officially promoted on January 1, 2026. MDs at BNY are senior leaders who help us drive our company forward and execute our strategy.

This year’s class reflects a strong track record of innovation, measurable client impact and commercial focus, and includes a range of remarkable individual stories. These colleagues are helping to drive BNY forward and position the company at the forefront of financial markets.

“Our new Managing Directors exemplify what it means to be client obsessed, to spark progress, and to own outcomes,” said Shannon Hobbs, Chief People Officer, BNY. “Their leadership will help us run our company better and power our culture across all of our global locations.”
