Senior Specialist, Full-Stack Engineer (APAC AI Hub)

We’re seeking a future team member for the role of Senior Specialist, Full-Stack Engineer to join our APAC AI Hub team. To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following:Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience, is required.Experience, preferably 3+ years, in software engineering, applied AI/ML, application development, or a related technical discipline.Strong software-development fundamentals and practical experience building, testing, and maintaining backend services, APIs, applications, or integrations.Familiarity with Python and modern software-development frameworks, source control, automated testing, CI/CD, and cloud-based development environments.Foundational knowledge of LLMs, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, session context management, model APIs, machine learning, or related AI technologies.Ability to troubleshoot technical issues, learn unfamiliar technologies quickly, and produce clear, maintainable code and documentation.Strong teamwork and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate across technical and non-technical teams in APAC and other global locations.Experience in financial services or another regulated industry is a plus.
