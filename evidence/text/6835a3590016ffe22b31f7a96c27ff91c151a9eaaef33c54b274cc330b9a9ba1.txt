BNY was honored with three awards at the 2025 Leaders in Custody Awards, hosted by Global Custodian on May 15 at The Savoy in London. The annual event recognizes outstanding achievements across the securities services industry, spotlighting firms that demonstrate excellence in client service, innovation, and operational leadership.
BNY’s Asset Servicing business won the Asset Servicing Partnership of the Year award for its collaboration with BlackRock, to support a major Asian insurance group in enhancing their investment platform with a new data-driven operating model to elevate client experience and operational efficiency.
Emily Portney, Global Head of Asset Servicing, received the prestigious Lifetime Achievement Award, for her decades-long leadership and influence across capital markets and custody services, as well as her commitment to innovation and client success.
The firm’s Markets – Global Collateral Platform was also recognized, winning Securities Finance / Collateral Initiative of the Year for its work with Pirum on ECPOConnect, a joint collateral optimization solution allowing for enhanced operational efficiency and visibility.
BNY’s continued success at the Global Custodian awards highlights its industry leadership and investment in scalable, client-centric solutions.
