Senior Software Developer Engineer in Testing

Position Summary: The Clearing House (TCH) is seeking a Senior Software Developer Engineer in Testing (SDET) to join our RTP Product Engineering team. As a senior member of the team, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and executing automated and manual test cases, ensuring quality of our critical real-time interbank payment platform. This includes functionality, reliability and performance of existing and new applications. The Senior SDET works directly with RTP Product teams and Operations to aggregate business and technical requirements into high quality automation testing of TCH Systems. Develop and deliver testing best practices and processes to all projects while maintaining adherence to TCH standards.

This role is well suited for an experienced engineer who enjoys solving complex technical problems, testing highly reliable systems, and delivering quality applications that operate at the core of financial infrastructure. The organization is actively transforming its payment platforms toward cloud-native, distributed architectures while introducing modern automation and AI-assisted engineering practices into its software development lifecycle.

TCH values engineers who are technically curious, pragmatic, and interested in understanding how complex systems work, end to end. The ideal candidate will be a hands-on quality engineer who has strong automation experience, understands distributed application architectures, and an avid interest in testing critical systems where performance, security, resiliency, and sustainability are vital for success.

Essential Functions and Responsibilities:

Lead planning, estimation, development, and implementation of test automation frameworks and strategies

Design and develop automated testing including creation, execution, and maintenance of automated scripts using industry best practices and standards

Execute translation of manual testing scenarios to test automation coverage across multiple products

Participate either as the sole QE resource on projects, or in an Agile scrum team

Use of strong problem-solving skills to investigate and document defects and their respective resolutions

Lead larger test automation efforts independently and act as a subject matter expert to team members on smaller efforts

Effectively communicate status to stakeholders and proactively locate and address concerns

Champion Agile best-practices, processes, and tools in support of DevOps processes with respect to developing testing automation

Conform to department standards to comply with audit and regulatory policies

Assist team members through cross-training on applications and systems, perform peer reviews, training and mentoring manual and junior QE members on automation testing

Participate in the transformation of testing into a continuous and efficient end-to-end quality engineering function

Aid in the evolution of more predictive and intelligent testing approaches based on automation

Collaborate with other test engineers across products and projects to solve problems and deploy them, and mature automation testing practices

About The Clearing House: The Clearing House (TCH) is a banking association and payments company that is owned by the largest commercial banks in the United States. TCH owns and operates the core payments system infrastructure in the US and is currently working to modernize that infrastructure by launching a new, ubiquitous, real-time payment system. TCH is the only private-sector ACH and wire operator in the US, clearing and settling nearly $2 trillion in US dollar payments each day, representing half of all commercial ACH and wire volume. TCH has provided critical services to the banking industry since 1853, and today, our core products facilitate services including Direct Deposit of payroll, bill payments, and check image exchange.
