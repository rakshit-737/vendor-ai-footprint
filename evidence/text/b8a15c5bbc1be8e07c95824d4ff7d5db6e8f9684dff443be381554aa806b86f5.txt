Conventional Debt Instruments

Conventional Debt Instruments

Independent trust and agency services supporting conventional debt issuance, settlement and administration across global markets

OUR VALUE

BNY provides services for conventional debt from pre-issuance through maturity, helping clients execute financing strategies and meet obligations. Product-aligned teams combine specialized expertise and global infrastructure to support efficient administration of public and private debt structures operating under a variety of governing laws and enable operational resilience.

PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS

Key Agency Roles

We act in core roles including trustee, fiscal agent, issuing and paying agent, registrar, transfer agent, calculation agent, account bank, custodian, escrow agent and security agent.

Global Market Access

Connectivity to selected international clearing systems, including Euroclear, Clearstream and DTCC, supports efficient issuance, settlement and ongoing servicing across jurisdictions.

Broad Instrument Support

We support a wide range of conventional debt instruments, including stand-alone bonds, medium-term notes, high-yield bonds, commercial paper, certificates of deposit and convertible bonds.

BENEFITS AT A GLANCE

Independent Oversight

As a neutral intermediary, BNY acts in accordance with transaction documentation, supporting transparency, consistency and confidence for all transaction parties.

Operational Reliability

Standardized processes, resilient infrastructure and disciplined controls support timely execution, accurate administration and dependable servicing from issuance through maturity.

Scalable Support

Experience supporting high volumes of debt issuances across diversified product classes enables scalable solutions that adapt to evolving transaction needs and market conditions.
