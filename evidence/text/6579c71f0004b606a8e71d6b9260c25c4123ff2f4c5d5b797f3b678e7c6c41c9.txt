Vice President, AI Learning & Enablement

Role SummaryOwn the live AI learning experience from intake to wrap-up. Orchestrate AI Bootcamps and live enablement programs: align speakers and topics; confirm AI Hub mentor availability; secure venues/virtual rooms; manage invitations and nominations; and ensure transparent communications with participants and managers. Collaborate with AI Hub and L&LD to co-create hands-on tasks, capstones, and rubric-based assessments. Safeguard reporting accuracy, satisfaction, and success tracking across events.Key Responsibilities• AI Bootcamp ownership: Plan and deliver multi-day Bootcamps end-to-end (agenda, speaker lineup, mentor staffing, locations/virtual setup, run-of-show, recordings). Define capacity targets, throughput, and success measures per cohort.• Events portfolio: Produce and support AI webinars, hackathons, roadshows, and local/virtual enablement sessions; create facilitator kits, producer checklists, and day-of roles.• Speakers & mentors: Align topics and speakers; confirm AI Hub mentor availability and ratios; brief presenters with goals, timeboxes, and demo scripts; run technical checks.• Participant operations: Drive transparent communications to nominees, participants, and managers (invites, prep packets, prerequisites, expectations, logistics); manage waitlists and substitutions.• Cohort formation: Coordinate timely nomination intake with AI champions/coordinators; gather project ideas and team lists; validate prerequisites and diversity of roles/locations.• On-the-day excellence: Host, time-keep, and quality-assure sessions; triage blockers; capture Q&A, FAQs, and artifacts for reuse.• Success tracking: Ensure accurate attendance, completion, capstone outcomes, and certification pass/fail tracking; publish cohort recaps and insights.• Catalog hygiene: Ensure recordings, slides, and artifacts are tagged and published to the right repositories (e.g., LXP/LMS, SharePoint), following brand, accessibility, and governance standards.Required Skills & Experience• Production & facilitation: End-to-end live event production (virtual and in-person); run-of-show creation; presenter/mentor enablement; troubleshooting A/V & platforms.• Program operations: Cohort building, nomination intake, communications plans, logistics (rooms/catering/virtual), risk and contingency planning.• Curriculum & assessment: Designing hands-on tasks, labs, rubrics, and capstones; partnering with instructional design teams to ensure outcomes alignment.• Platforms & data: Expertise with Teams/Stream, SharePoint, Forms, Saba/Oracle, Degreed, and analytics dashboards (e.g., Power BI) for attendance, completion, NPS, and proficiency signals.
