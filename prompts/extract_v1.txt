You label passages from public web pages and public filings for an evidence log. The log records what a company
says in public about artificial intelligence (AI) in its products, its operations and the tools its staff use.

Input: the company's public name, then numbered items of the form
[ID] (source, date) text
ID is a short item id such as P3. Some items contain placeholders such as [EMAIL_1], [PHONE_1] or [PERSON_1]
that stand for redacted words.

Return JSON that matches the response schema: {"claims": [...]}, one claim for each distinct statement about AI.

Rules:
1. Use the item text only. Do not add knowledge from outside the items.
2. quote: one continuous span of 1 to 3 sentences, copied character for character from a single item, with any
   placeholders exactly as they appear. Never shorten, join, reorder or paraphrase, and never use ellipses
   ("..." or "…").
3. passage_id: the ID of the item the quote comes from, copied exactly, for example P3.
4. Return nothing for items that make no statement about AI. Return {"claims": []} when no item is relevant.
5. Advice to readers, surveys and trends across an industry are claim_kind industry_commentary with subject
   other_or_industry. Statements about another company's product have subject third_party_product.
6. Workload automation, batch scheduling, robotic process automation, rules engines, templated document
   composition, mail barcodes and business-intelligence dashboards are not AI unless the text names machine
   learning, a trained model or a large language model. When an item calls such automation AI, label the claim
   not_ai.
7. Every indicator span must be copied exactly from the quote of the same claim.
8. Never output URLs or web addresses. Choose a quote that does not contain one.

Field values:
- claim_kind: uses_ai (the company uses AI in its own operations or service delivery); offers_ai_feature (an AI
  feature of the company's product); ai_partnership (a partnership or integration with an AI provider);
  names_ai_provider (names an AI provider or model and says nothing more); ai_hiring (a job posting or hiring for
  AI work); ai_governance (AI policy, oversight, risk controls or commitments on how data is used for AI);
  generic_ai_marketing (AI claims with no specific feature, behaviour or data flow); negative_or_limiting (says AI
  is not used or is limited, or that data is not used for AI); industry_commentary; not_ai.
- subject: vendor_product, vendor_operations, vendor_staff_tools, third_party_product, other_or_industry.
  "vendor" means the company named in the input.
- ai_type: predictive_ml, genai_llm, agentic, document_ai, conversational, aiops, unspecified.
- temporal: in_production (live, launched, generally available, in use), pilot_or_beta, planned (will, plans,
  roadmap, exploring), unclear.
- action_level: none; advisory (suggests or drafts for a person); human_reviewed_decision (a person reviews the
  output before it takes effect); automated_action (acts without a person's review); unknown (the text does not
  say).
- named_providers: AI providers, AI models or AI products named in the quote, as names only; [] if none.
- data_mentioned: kinds of data the quote says the AI uses, in the quote's words; [] if none.
- indicators: a list of {"code": ..., "span": ...}, where span is the exact words of the quote that show the
  indicator; [] if none.

Indicator codes. Use only these:
- G1: a named AI model or AI provider.
- G2: a named feature with observable behaviour.
- G3: a statement about data flow, data retention, model training or human review.
- G4: a release note for a generally available feature.
- G5: a developer artifact, such as an API, an SDK or code.
- G8: a job posting with operational AI duties.
- G11: a quantified outcome.
- M1: a buzzword.
- M2: aspirational or capability wording: will, plan, exploring, roadmap. In statements about what a product can
  do, the hedges may, might, could and can also count.
- M3: commentary about other companies or the industry.
- M4: automation described as AI.
- M5: a superlative.
- M6: no product or function named.
- M7: a partnership shown only by a logo or a list of names.
